## Problem Statement 
   Airport ground staff tasks are often managed manually, leading to delays, poor communication, and inefficient task tracking. This project provides a centralized platform to assign, monitor, and manage tasks efficiently, improving coordination and overall airport operations

## 1. Title
Airport Ground Staff Task Management Platform

## 2. Domain
Aviation Management / Airport Operations

## 3. Who is the User?
Admin – Manages users, staff, flights, and reports.
Supervisor – Assigns and monitors tasks.
Ground Staff – Views and updates assigned tasks.

## 4. What Problem Are We Solving?
Airport ground staff tasks are often managed manually using phone calls, paper records, or spreadsheets, which can lead to delays, poor communication, and missed assignments. Supervisors also find it difficult to track task progress in real time. This project provides a centralized platform to assign, monitor, and manage ground staff tasks efficiently, improving coordination and reducing operational delays.

## 5. Proposed Solution 
The application provides secure login, staff and flight management, task assignment, task tracking, report generation, and notifications. It enables supervisors to assign tasks efficiently and allows ground staff to update task status in real time.

## 7. User Roles & Permissions

## 8. Success Criteria 
A supervisor should be able to assign a task in under 1 minute.
Ground staff should be able to update task status instantly.
Admin should be able to generate reports quickly.
